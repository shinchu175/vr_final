Goa

Goa is a small Indian state on the west coast known for its beaches, Portuguese heritage, vibrant culture, and tourism-driven economy.

Best time: Nov - March

Bali

Bali, known as the "Island of the Gods," is an Indonesian province renowned for its rich Hindu culture, scenic landscapes, diverse temples, and tourism-driven economy.

Best time: April - October

Maldives

The Maldives is a tropical island nation in the Indian Ocean, renowned for its 26 atolls, over 1,200 coral islands, turquoise waters, and luxury tourism, making it one of the world's most unique and widely dispersed countries.

Best time: Nov - April

Dubai

Dubai is a global city and business hub in the United Arab Emirates (UAE), known for its futuristic skyscrapers, luxury lifestyle, and vibrant cultural and economic significance.

Best time: Nov - March

New York

New York City (NYC) is a vibrant metropolis known for its rich history, cultural diversity, and iconic landmarks, making it one of the most influential cities in the world.

Best time: Sep - Nov

London

London is the capital city of the United Kingdom and England, renowned for its rich history, diverse culture, and global economic significance.

Best time: May - June

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a world-renowned white marble mausoleum in Agra, India, celebrated for its stunning Mughal architecture and designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Best time: Oct - March

Colosseum

The Colosseum, also known as the Flavian Amphitheatre, is an ancient Roman amphitheatre renowned for its grandeur, history, and role in gladiatorial games.

Best time: Mar - May

Great Wall

The Great Wall of China is a vast series of fortifications built over centuries to protect northern China, stretching over 21,196 kilometers and recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Best time: Sep - Nov

Switzerland

Switzerland, officially known as the Swiss Confederation, is a landlocked country in Central Europe, renowned for its stunning landscapes, cultural diversity, and political neutrality.

Best time: June - September

Grand Canyon

The Grand Canyon is a world-renowned natural wonder in northern Arizona, USA, known for its dramatic landscapes, geological significance, and awe-inspiring vistas.

Best time: Apr - May

Northern Lights

The northern lights, or Aurora Borealis, are stunning natural light displays in the polar skies, caused by charged solar particles interacting with Earth's atmosphere

Best time: Aug - April
